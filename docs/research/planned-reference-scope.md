# What a planned reference proves

Review date: 2026-09-05. This reviews the frozen `doorbench.reference-motion.v1` corpus and the current `doorbench.planned-reference.v1` planner, independent validator and corpus runner. The two accepted pilot artifacts are `out/reference-planned-pilot/db0002_swing_single` and `db0079_sliding_single`. Their accepted status establishes the declared sampled **kinematic and actor-route checks**, not causal humanoid opening or unlocking.

The distinction matters even for a simple knob. In the frozen `db0002_swing_single` recording, 11 samples with the leaf still within 0.001 rad of closed and positive knob effort rotate the knob from 0.438887 to 0.839328 rad: **0.400441 rad (22.94°)**. The recorded hand target moves at most **1.49e-8 m** on any axis. Following this XYZ point does not demonstrate turning the wrist with the knob.

## Evidence retained by the frozen source

The [scripted-hand policy](../../doorbench/benchmark/baselines/scripted_hand.py) is an oracle with access to joint names, lock parts, release permissions and keypad codes. It directly commands generalized efforts for lock parts, operators and leaves. The [runner](../../doorbench/benchmark/runner.py) adds these efforts to `qfrc_applied`; it does not obtain them from simulated humanoid hand contact. Multiple lock parts and both leaves can receive efforts in the same step.

The [recorder](../../doorbench/reference/record.py), `Recorder.__call__`, retains native joint positions, velocities, generalized efforts, controls, body positions/quaternions, the synthetic base and one world XYZ target. It selects the target from the highest-priority actuated hardware, preferring lock/operator joints over the leaf. The selected target joint/site/geom identity, surface normal, wrist quaternion, palm/finger approach and grasp frame are **not retained**. Native body orientations can reconstruct hardware orientation, but cannot recover a missing intended hand orientation or prove a grasp.

`badge` and `declare_locked` survive as source episode events, not a full actor action/contact schedule. `DoorBenchEnv.badge()` releases a lock through an API; the event is not necessarily evidence of a physical card and reader. For example, `db0033_gate_sliding` has a slide bolt yet records a `badge` event. Keypad key joints and their motion do survive in native state, but the source oracle's joint effort is not a recorded fingertip press. A source `touch_handle` label can also be generated by operator travel, so it is not independently measured humanoid contact.

## What the new pipeline adds, and what remains supplied

[guidance.py](../../doorbench/reference/guidance.py) authors an approach, working stances, footsteps, reach/release blends and a proposed route using the source door trajectory. [solve.py](../../doorbench/reference/solve.py) solves the fixed adult rig around those native poses. [validate_planned_reference.py](../../scripts/validate_planned_reference.py) recompiles the original rig and source door and independently checks recorded FK, actor joint limits, sampled derivatives, ground support, stance drift, hand target residual, permitted contact pairs, collision clearance and subdivided interpolation. It separately checks the actor pelvis crosses the declared aperture and reaches the goal for traversal scenarios.

Those are useful advances over the published avatar. They leave the following semantic limits:

- **Hands are position targets.** `targets_at` supplies XYZ and positional cost for hands, without `quat_wxyz`. The rig has wrist rotation DOFs, but spherical 35 mm-radius hands and no articulated fingers. Joint limits alone do not couple wrist orientation to a knob, lever, thumbturn or wheel.
- **Contact is a geometric proxy.** The current resolver chooses a nearby visible primitive surface, rather than a recorded source grasp identity. Its nominal hand surface gap is 4.5 mm, inside the validator's 5 mm contact-distance tolerance. A permitted hand/geom pair and small point residual do not prove literal contact, a wrap/pinch grip, frictional force closure or transmitted torque.
- **One selected point does not cover an operation schedule.** The planner carries one selected hand through the source operation. Source efforts can act on multiple hardware parts at once; some may be mechanically coupled and some independently operated. Neither their complete manual sequence nor regrasp/bimanual coordination is established by the current target stream.
- **Native motion is prescribed.** The solver sets source door `qpos` for each actor frame. Retiming changes the source pose clock, without replaying its original forces or dynamics. The guide holds the selected final native pose while the actor traverses and settles. A closer, overhead leaf, automatic timeout or safety sensor therefore needs an explicit support/power/control explanation before this becomes causal execution.
- **Task timing and task semantics are separate.** The accepted knob pilot lasts **89.27 s** against its source scenario's **30 s** budget; the sliding pilot lasts **65.82 s** against **35 s**. The validator currently does not enforce that budget. Its actor-route evidence also does not re-evaluate force/damage labels, sensing or lock recognition on the new actor clock. `locked_recognize` uses the declared source result and expressly does not certify recognition semantics.

Consequently, neither accepted pilot is evidence of a humanoid policy solving the original timed benchmark. The door recordings retain their own oracle benchmark outcomes. Changing the clock can make a reference easier to track while changing timeout, delayed-egress, powered-door and damage behavior; the original dynamic outcome cannot be transferred to that clock.

## Minimum evidence for stronger claims

**Grasp-aware kinematic operation** needs a versioned operation schedule identifying each actuated joint/geom, its allowed approach side, the assigned hand, and approach/grasp/release/regrasp intervals. For a rigid grip, record a local SE(3) hand-to-hardware frame with a meaningful palm/finger convention and check the relative position **and orientation** throughout the operation. Specify intended rotation axis/direction/travel or linear push direction. For knobs/wheels, demonstrate compatible wrist/arm rotation and explicit regrasp where continuous rotation exceeds the rig's limits. A sphere's rotational symmetry cannot stand in for a human hand.

For locks and multiple parts, verify the full release ordering and mechanism state: retract every required dog/bolt, preserve coupled link behavior, return/release momentary controls, and complete the keypad press/release sequence when required. Credential/key releases need a declared available credential and an actor-generated action at the correct device/side. Powered doors need an actor-generated trigger plus sensor/safety timing. Recognition needs evidence of the bounded probe, observation and declaration; merely retaining the source declaration is insufficient. These checks can still be kinematic, but must be labeled that way.

**Causal physical humanoid operation** additionally requires a simulated or measured execution in which allowed actor actuators and contacts cause the manual hardware/leaf motion. Remove direct oracle forces and prescribed native joint poses for those parts; model and log grasp/contact wrenches, friction, actuator effort/range, support and balance. Explicitly modeled motors may drive powered doors, with their command and safety states logged. Re-evaluate all original task criteria, damage/slam constraints, event order and time budget on the executed actor clock. A free pelvis trajectory with a soft COM objective is not a balance or force certificate.

Keep distinct fields or states for `kinematic_validation`, `grasp_operation_validation`, `causal_execution_validation`, `benchmark_result` and human visual review. Until stronger gates exist, suitable wording is: **“Kinematic route verified. Door motion supplied by a scripted-hand recording; grasp and causal operation are unverified.”** Avoid “humanoid success”, “physically solved”, or “validated unlocking” based only on `accepted_kinematic`.

## Intrinsic obstruction versus planner limits

A geometric impossibility claim needs evidence for the specified fixed body and permitted task, not a failed local solve. The new rig's rigid head is a **0.20 m-diameter sphere**. Seven pet-door openings are narrower: `db0238_pet_door`, `db0627_pet_door`, `db0892_pet_door` are 0.19 m wide; `db0743_pet_door`, `db0753_pet_door`, `db0901_pet_door`, `db0951_pet_door` are 0.16 m wide. That head cannot pass those fixed rectangular apertures without collision. This is an obstruction for this rig and geometry, not a universal statement about humans, other embodiments or larger pet doors.

A standing-route search failure, finite search extent, local IK convergence failure, poor initialization, aggressive timing, missing regrasp/bimanual planning or absent climbing/crawling support planner is **unresolved/unsupported**, not proof of impossibility. Source-oracle failure is also not a human impossibility proof. Missing permission or a physically inaccessible release may preclude a particular task under its declared rules, but that needs a task-specific argument. Do not silently shrink limbs, remove colliders, unlock hardware or replace traversal with a different goal to obtain acceptance.

## Independent corpus-runner review

The runner requires complete proposals, successful declared source episodes, matching source/output hashes, independent `accepted` and `kinematic_accepted`, task evidence and no failure counts/file. It rechecks source/generator/runtime identity, publishes fresh attempts, and verifies artifact inventories/checksums when resuming. A successful source episode or solver diagnostic alone does not promote a candidate.

The review found a provenance gap in the initial runner: it hashed whatever source NPZ was supplied without checking the frozen reference index. The owner fixed it before corpus expansion: the runner now binds the index bytes, paths, clip/trajectory checksums, sources, scenario and outcome into preparation and identity. This closes replacement of a trajectory while keeping an unrelated frozen success declaration. The reviewed final runner contains no identified path that promotes partial, independently rejected or stale artifacts. This is a local integrity check; it is not an external signature or renewed source benchmark execution.

No generator, validator, renderer, source recording or asset was changed by this semantic review. Added artifacts are this scope note, the read-only inventory script and focused inventory tests. The inventory does not add mechanism acceptance gates; those remain explicit future work.

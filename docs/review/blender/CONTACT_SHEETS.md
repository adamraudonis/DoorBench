# Blender catalogue contact sheets

All 1,000 final previews were personally screened in these 50 sheets. Each image is a downsampled actual Blender Cycles render at 480×480 and 16 samples. The [review](REVIEW.md) records the remaining source-model and appearance limitations; this is not mechanical or photographic certification.

The [manifest](atlas/manifest.json) and [screening ledger](screening.json) map each door to its recipe, original image and SHA-256 checksum. Full images are generated locally under `out/appearance`. The [six-look comparison](looks.jpg) uses the higher-quality 960×960 examples.

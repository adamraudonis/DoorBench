# Physical turnstile mechanisms

The rebuilt 20 turnstiles use a physical reverse pawl where one-way rotation is declared, plus a separate spring-return index bolt. Their primary rotor joints are unlimited. Credential input energizes the bolt's actual linear armature; it does not disable a rotor limit or overwrite a pose. The six declared tripod drop-arm variants have a separate physical, manually restored release mechanism; their three indexed positions pass the native repeated-cycle proof in all tiers.

The inventory is ten tripod and ten full-height doors: 17 one-way, three bidirectional, 13 initially credential-locked, and seven initially powered/unlocked. The bidirectional cases are DB0273, DB0497 and DB0880. The six declared drop-arm cases are DB0202, DB0272, DB0344, DB0393, DB0516 and DB0946.

The current catalogue now identifies all 20 devices as a fail-secure `turnstile_index_bolt`, including the seven initially energized examples. It carries zero additional catalogue lock mass because the wheel, pawl and bolt already have explicit geometry-based mass. This replaces 13 old `mag_lock` labels and seven `none` labels after all random draws. An exact old/new generation comparison verifies that all other fields of all 1,000 specifications are unchanged, including the 13 engaged locks, unavailable credentials, and original task labels. Their authored task labels are not evidence that a locked traversal is feasible.

## Physical construction and scope

The fixed mechanism base, bearing stays, solenoid guides and end cap connect to the cabinet bearing or cage roof. The moving gear, pawl and bolt retain their authored steel mass and inertia; fixed housings are excluded from the moving-mass budget. The fixed geometry and two world-mounted moving members carry explicit world rest transforms. This avoids relying on a static wrapper to supply their pose.

On one-way doors, 36 separate convex teeth lift a sprung pivot pawl in the permitted direction. Reverse loading seats the pawl heel against a fixed stop, giving a tooth → nose → heel → stop → frame load path. An annular eye clears that stop throughout the lift range. The independent index wheel has one slot per passage sector. A physical bolt enters the slot and blocks either direction until its coil withdraws it by 22 mm. The guide has a 0.5 mm running gap; zero-clearance squeeze is rejected rather than hidden by a release threshold change.

The ideal coil law is `30 / (1 + remaining_gap / 0.040)^2` newtons, applied only to the actual bolt slide. The bolt has a 1000 N/m return spring, physical inertia and a modeled end stop. These dimensions and force ratings are authored engineering parameters. They are not manufacturer CAD, an OEM performance match, a structural-strength calculation, or an egress/safety certification. The native time step is 0.25 ms. Raw MJCF/other simulator imports require the corresponding coil input implementation to reproduce powered operation; static geometry and metadata alone do not supply it.

The manufacturer's documented topology supports this interpretation. Alvarado's EDC manual shows separate reversing pawls, lock arms and solenoid plungers on printed page 16. Its printed page 11 describes a separate optional horizontal-arm drop function, which requires manual restoration after a fire input clears. That function must not be inferred from successful rotation-lock tests. [Alvarado EDC installation and maintenance instructions](https://www.alvaradomfg.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/PUD1535R6-0_SEC_WTS_EDC-Installation-Maintenance-Guide.pdf).

## Direction/credential stage evidence, before drop-arm integration

All 20 doors in full, simple and minimal tiers pass the same four finite-load controls: powered/unpowered × forward/reverse 80 Nm load. The controls require actual named index-bolt contacts for credential arrest, actual tooth and fixed-stop contacts for reverse arrest, physical pawl lift during forward travel, observed bolt withdrawal and at least one passage-sector rotation when allowed. They check every simulation step for contact penetration, excessive bolt travel, nonfinite states and native warnings. All 60 cases pass; the largest measured contact penetration is 0.65004 mm against the unchanged 1 mm limit.

The seven focused mechanism tests also cover missing pawl/bolt counterexamples, a detached coil end cap, fixed-versus-moving mass, world-frame preservation, passive-only inspection and observed default coil withdrawal. They pass in 349.13 seconds. Five separate rotor tests pass in 30.43 seconds, including surface direction/contact across all 35 revolving/turnstile doors and all three tiers, broken supports/axes, physical credential arrest, and native opening from force applied at an actual surface site.

DB0272 reaches 21.5 mm bolt travel after 24 ms of unloaded power and settles at 22.00028 mm by 50 ms. Its peak transient penetration is 0.41242 mm and native warnings are zero. Under load, a de-energized bolt may arrest a rotor before fully reseating; remaining stroke is not equivalent to geometric engagement depth. Arrest must be supported by the actual index-sector contact and angle.

The clearance inspector keeps the rotor and electrical input fixed while a private native copy settles only the original passive pawl. It copies the original rigid boundary, mass, inertia, joint properties and mesh vertices, verifies the copied geometry within 2 μm, and rejects unsettled, penetrated or out-of-range results. Blocked sweeps end at the first named physical arrest; released sweeps remain separate. Ratchet sweeps use at least 144 samples per full revolution to cover intermediate tooth phases. This is a quasistatic geometry inspection, not a force-driven traversal certificate.

All 20 final-source doors pass both dense penetration and running-clearance gates. `stage-receipt.json` binds the 60 native input files, owned source modules, the rotating builder function, and the completed evidence files with SHA-256 hashes.

Generated evidence is isolated under `out/mechanical-foundations/turnstile-locks/`: `pytest-physical-final/native-turnstiles0/native-proof.json`, `tests-physical-final.log`, `tests-rotating-final.log`, `coil-withdrawal.json` and `final-clearance/`. Earlier fixtures and interrupted test logs are retained as development evidence and are not the final source-bound proof.

## Indexed drop-arm construction and current verification

The six declared tripod variants now have three separately hinged hollow tubes. Each has a sprung transverse catch supporting a raised toe, an actual hinge eye/pin and a physical 90° fold stop. One cabinet-supported release shoe reaches the currently indexed horizontal arm. Its dedicated solenoid supplies force to hold the shoe clear; power loss lets a spring move the shoe against that arm's catch. Gravity folds the released arm. Power restoration withdraws the shoe but leaves the arm down. A force applied to the underside grip raises the arm, and the catch's diagonal underside permits mechanical recapture. The other two catches remain engaged.

The whole bearing and credential assembly is 100 mm farther rearward on these six variants, with the real journal extended by the same amount. This clears the falling tube without removing its support base. The drop mechanism has its own connected cabinet cantilever. Tube length is the authored radial reach minus the 65 mm hinge offset; exact tube mass transfers out of the rotor material budget, while catches, pins, brackets and solenoid retain their separate geometry-based mass.

The extended journal is also an explicit geometry-based body, so its 2.17113 kg steel mass is preserved rather than scaled with the calibrated rotor hub. Its former 2.5 kg catalogue rotary-bearing allowance is replaced; the fixed bearing housing remains outside moving assembly mass. The initial 18-case native proof preceded this accounting correction and remains interim evidence. The final 18-case proof uses the corrected inertia and catalogue inputs.

MuJoCo ordinarily filters contacts between directly joined parent/child bodies. Named contact pairs explicitly enable the arm/carrier fold stops and catch return flanges against their guides; global filtering remains unchanged. An independent distance check also caught a mounting web intruding into the catch stem bore. The web now terminates below that bore and connects to its surrounding guide walls. No clearance exceptions hide either defect.

This topology is consistent with the independent drop solenoid and manually restored horizontal arm documented in Alvarado's EDC manual, and with ZKTeco's separate drop/rotation-lock mechanisms. The latter also instructs manual arm restoration after power returns. These sources support the topology, not the authored dimensions, coil rating or timing. [ZKTeco TS2200 series user manual, printed pages 8–10](https://www.zkteco-ea.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/TS2200SeriesUserManual.pdf).

The corrected source passes 18 native cases: six doors × full/simple/minimal tiers, each with three indexed positions and two complete power-loss/restoration/manual-reset cycles per position. All 108 cycles pass, with maximum penetration 0.27875 mm and zero native warnings. Cycle checks require real catch, release-shoe and fold-stop contact loads, retain the other arms, limit applied reset force to 30 N and reject contact penetration over 1 mm. They do not write coordinates during a cycle. The reset grip is on the actual tube underside, not its centerline.

The same source also passes all 60 direction/credential cases and their 240 force probes after the bearing relocation and mass correction, with maximum penetration 0.64627 mm and zero warnings. All 20 final-source doors export successfully and pass the complete source QA, including their applicable native mechanism, dense penetration and running-clearance gates. All 60 final native files are byte-for-byte identical to the completed direction/credential force-test inputs. The 18 drop proof files also match; their IR differs only by removing the completed mechanism’s temporary incomplete-status message.

Other controls verify the fixed support chain, actual grip surface and moment direction, tube/journal mass in every tier, passive-only runtime behavior, invalid axis/stroke rejection and unchanged credential/task metadata. A detached end-cap negative uses the actual mechanism frame, including the rotated six-drop layout. Five independent rotor tests pass across the 35 revolving/turnstile fixtures.

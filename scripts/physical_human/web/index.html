A SMALL, INSPECTABLE PROTOTYPE

This is a custom simple lever door and a controller-driven human approximation. The body and fingers are simulated rigid bodies; the motion is authored, not captured from a person. This demonstration covers standing door opening—not walking through, a trained policy or validated human biomechanics.

SYNTHETIC REFERENCE · ONE SIMPLE DOOR

A MyoHand-derived skeleton, compliant contact envelopes and a floating body run in native MuJoCo. An authored reach and arm controller combines with eight finger corrections selected through 64 simulated policy-search trials. Real door-opening footage guided the grasp. This is an engineered simulation, not captured human ground truth or a validated biomechanical model. The demonstrated scope is standing opening and hold.

The previous v2 recording was rejected because the thumb did not touch the lever. It is replaced here by v3 with explicit side, contact and arm-continuity checks.

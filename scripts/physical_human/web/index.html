A SMALL, INSPECTABLE PROTOTYPE

This is a custom simple lever door and a controller-driven human approximation. The hand uses MyoHand joint geometry with COCO-WholeBody landmark ordering. Thin bones enforce skeleton separation; overlapping tissue envelopes contact the environment. The motion and masses are authored approximations. This demonstration covers standing door opening—not walking through, a trained policy or validated human biomechanics.
